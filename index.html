04 / WHO WE ARE

INDEPENDENT BY NATURE.
BUILT TO KEEP MOVING.

Kloz Studio is an independent game development studio founded by KLOUTYN. We focus on Unity and on the genres that inspire us most: FPS, survival and multiplayer games.

We're at the beginning of the journey. Our focus is on learning, prototyping, testing and building a foundation for original games. As the studio grows, we hope to collaborate with more creators and turn ambitious ideas into playable experiences.
